03 · HOW I THINK

A question becomes a decision.

The method changes with the problem. The discipline underneath it stays consistent.

01

Understand the question

Clarify the decision, the people using it, and what a useful answer would change.

02

Understand the data

Trace the source, grain, owner, definitions, and practical limits of the evidence.

03

Validate

Check completeness, consistency, duplicates, date logic, and reconciliation before trusting the output.

04

Analyze

Explore patterns and root causes, then choose the simplest method that fits the question.

05

Communicate

Make the conclusion, uncertainty, and next decision easy to understand.

06

Measure

Look for time saved, trust gained, manual effort removed, and better decisions.
